# Join a community

Joining a Betat community means becoming a **Provenancier** — a verified
human contributor whose knowledge enters the permanent record. The
community verifies that you are a real person, then you can submit
content for review. When a verifier accepts your submission, it becomes
a provenance record carrying the HI tag: permanently attributed to you,
permanently readable by anyone.

You do not need to install anything. You need a browser and an invitation
or enrollment link from your community.

---

## How joining works

**1. Find your community's Betat instance.**
Your community administrator will give you the URL — something like
`archive.example.org` or similar. This is your community's own Betat
site, not betat.org.

**2. Enroll as a Provenancier.**
Visit the Enroll page on your community's site. You will provide a
display name and authenticate through your community's chosen method
— peer vouching, a cryptographic key, or an institutional identity.
Your community verifies that you are a real, known human before
enrollment is complete.

**3. Submit your first record.**
Once enrolled, you receive a submission token. Use it to submit
content for review. Provide a persistent link to the content and a
hash — your community administrator or the documentation can help
with this if it is unfamiliar.

**4. A verifier reviews your submission.**
A community verifier reviews what you submitted. If accepted, your
submission becomes a provenance record: permanent, public, and
carrying the HI tag.

---

## What you are declaring

When you submit content, you accept a declaration:

> *I declare that this content was originated by a human being. I am
> that human being, or I am an authorized representative. I understand
> that this declaration is permanent, public, and append-only — it
> cannot be removed or modified.*

This declaration is the foundation of the HI tag. It is what makes
a Betat provenance record meaningful. The permanence is not a legal
threat — it is a commitment to the record's integrity. A record that
can be quietly removed or changed is not a record worth trusting.

---

## What you do not need

- An account on betat.org
- Any software installed on your device
- Technical knowledge beyond using a web form

Betat is designed so that the knowledge matters, not the technology.
If you can fill out a web form, you can contribute to the record.

---

## Reading records

Anyone can read provenance records — no enrollment, no account, no
cost. Reading is always free and always open. The record for anything
your community has verified is findable through your community's site
or through the Betat registry when it launches.
