# Betat — In Plain Words

*A welcome for every visitor. No technical knowledge needed. (Also available as [plain text](WELCOME.txt).)*

## What This Is About

Think about a child born today. By the time that child grows up, the
internet will be full of writing, pictures, voices, and ideas made by
machines. Much of it will look exactly like something a person made.

How will that child know what real people actually knew, created,
believed, and lived through?

Right now, there is no reliable way to tell. Betat exists to build one —
while it is still possible.


## The Idea, Simply

Think of an antique. A factory can make a copy that looks identical,
but the copy is not the antique. What makes the original valuable is
its story: a real person made it, at a real moment, in a real place.
That story of origin is called provenance.

Betat does for human knowledge what provenance does for antiques.
When a real person shares something — a scientist's observation, a
grandmother's story, an artist's work, a witness's account — Betat
records who made it, when, and how that was confirmed. That record is
kept permanently, and the content is marked with a small badge:

    HI — meaning Human Intelligence. A real, verified person made this.

Two letters. That is the whole promise. When you see HI, a human being
stands behind that content, and the record proves it.

If something has no HI badge, that does not mean it is fake or
machine-made. It simply means no one has confirmed its human origin
yet. Betat points to what is verified human — it does not accuse
anything else.


## What Betat Is Not

Betat is not a social network. It is not a fact-checker — it confirms
that a human made something, not whether that human was right. It does
not judge quality. It does not store or copy your content; your work
stays where it lives, and Betat keeps only the record of its origin.
And it is not owned by any company or government.


## Three Things Every Visitor Should Know

1. Reading is free, for everyone, forever.
   No account. No payment. No permission needed. Anyone, anywhere, can
   search and read what has been verified as human-made. That promise
   is permanent.

2. Adding content requires being verified as a real person.
   Groups of people — called communities — organize around what they
   know: science, art, faith, law, craft, personal stories, indigenous
   knowledge, and more. Each community confirms that its members are
   real human beings and that what they submit is genuinely
   human-made. A person verified this way is called a Provenancier
   (pro-VEN-an-see-ay) — a keeper of human origin.

3. Nothing is ever erased.
   Records are added, never deleted or rewritten. Mistakes are
   corrected by adding new records, not by hiding old ones. The human
   record — including its errors and its arguments — stays whole for
   whoever comes next.


## About The Name

Betat comes from the Bamileke language of the Bantu people of
Cameroon. It means "the other person" — humanhood. In English, TAT
also spells Turn Around Time: the time between a question and its
answer.

We are building this for the other person. Someone we will never
meet, in a future we cannot imagine, who deserves to know what human
beings really knew and lived — without waiting.


## Why This Cannot Wait

A record of origin must be made at the moment of creation. It cannot
be reconstructed later. We are living in the last stretch of time when
the line between human-made and machine-made is still clear. Every
year that passes without this record, the window narrows.


## How You Can Help

You do not need to be a programmer.

  - If you belong to a group that holds knowledge — teachers, elders,
    scientists, artists, faith communities, professional bodies — you
    can help start a community that verifies and records its own
    people's work.
  - If you have knowledge or experience worth preserving, you can
    become a Provenancier through a community in your domain.
  - If you speak more than one language, you can help translate these
    words so they reach everyone.
  - If you build software, the technical documents in this project
    (README, ARCHITECTURE, PROVENANCE_SPEC) explain exactly what to
    build first.
  - If you simply believe the record of human experience is worth
    keeping — share this. Telling one other person is itself a human
    act, and this is a project made of human acts.


## Who Started This

matter — the results do. This project is free and open, released so
that anyone can take it further than its originator imagined.
