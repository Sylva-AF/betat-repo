# Betat

A child born today will grow up in a world where it is increasingly impossible to tell whether something was created by a human being — or generated by a machine. Betat is an open framework for preserving the provenance of human-originated content: verified by communities of real people, tagged **HI** — Human Intelligence — and permanently findable by anyone, free, forever.

**We do not chase the fake. We prove the real.**
