# Find a community

Betat communities are organized around specific types of human knowledge.
A scientific community verifies field observations. An oral history
community verifies spoken testimony. A creative community verifies
works of human imagination. Each community decides its own standards,
within the Betat protocol floor.

If your knowledge belongs somewhere, there is — or will be — a community
for it.

## What makes a Betat community

Every Betat community:

- Verifies exactly one type of human-originated content
- Sets its own authentication standards, above the Betat baseline
- Issues provenance records that anyone can read, free, forever
- Is permanently identified by a domain it controls
- Cannot weaken its own standards once set — only strengthen them

A community's records are permanent. The HI tag, once issued, is part
of the record forever. This is why community identity and standards
matter: they are the trust behind every record the community issues.
