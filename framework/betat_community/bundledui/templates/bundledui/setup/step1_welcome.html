Getting started

Set up your Betat community

This wizard will configure your community instance in a few steps. You will declare your community's identity, what it verifies, and how members authenticate — the same decisions the Betat protocol requires every community to make once.

At the end, your instance will be ready for members to enroll, submit content, and receive verified provenance records.

What you will need:

A domain name you control (e.g. archive.example.org)
A name and description for your community
The URI where your records will be published
A contact email address
